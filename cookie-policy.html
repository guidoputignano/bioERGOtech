Cookie Policy

Last updated: February 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and understand how you interact with it. Some cookies are essential for the website to function, while others help us improve your experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

The bioERGOtech Foundation website (bioergotech.org) uses cookies and similar technologies in two categories:

2.1 Strictly Necessary (Always Active)

These are essential for the website to function and cannot be disabled. They include:

Technology Purpose Storage Duration
Supabase Auth Member portal authentication (login session) localStorage Until logout
Cookie Consent Remembers your cookie preferences localStorage Persistent

2.2 Analytics (Requires Consent)

These cookies help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. They are only set if you accept analytics cookies.

Cookie Provider Purpose Duration
_ga Google Analytics Distinguishes unique visitors 2 years
_gid Google Analytics Distinguishes unique visitors 24 hours
_gat Google Analytics Throttles request rate 1 minute

We use Google Analytics with IP anonymisation enabled (anonymize_ip: true), which means your full IP address is never stored by Google.

3. Third-Party Services

Our website loads resources from the following external services:

  • Google Fonts — Loads the Poppins typeface. Google may log font requests. Google Privacy Policy
  • Supabase — Provides authentication and database services for the member portal. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • jsDelivr CDN — Delivers CSS and JavaScript libraries (Tailwind CSS, FontAwesome). No tracking.
  • UI Avatars — Generates avatar images from initials for the member directory. No cookies.

4. Managing Your Preferences

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the cookie icon in the bottom-left corner of any page. You can also:

  • Delete cookies through your browser settings
  • Block cookies in your browser (note: this may affect site functionality)
  • Use browser extensions to control tracking

6. Legal Basis

We process cookie data under the following legal bases under the GDPR:

  • Strictly necessary cookies: Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — required for the website to function
  • Analytics cookies: Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — only set after you explicitly opt in

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:
