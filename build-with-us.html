Your Journey With Us

A staged progression from idea to company. Enter at whatever stage matches where you are today.

1

Validate

3 — 6 months

Test your concept against real clinical needs. We help you determine whether your idea solves a meaningful problem before you commit to building a company.

  • Clinical need assessment with our healthcare network
  • Technical feasibility review by our scientific team
  • Market landscape analysis and competitive positioning
  • Mentorship from domain experts and entrepreneurs

For: researchers with promising ideas, academics exploring commercialization, teams at concept stage.

2

Incubate

6 — 12 months

Build your company with dedicated resources. We provide the infrastructure, team, and strategic support to go from validated concept to minimum viable product.

  • Access to laboratory facilities and technical infrastructure
  • Dedicated mentorship from biotech and AI experts
  • Regulatory and compliance guidance
  • Introductions to our investor network
  • Spin-off support and company formation

For: validated teams ready to build, spin-offs from our research partnerships, BioShot competition winners.

3

Accelerate

12+ months

Scale to market with strategic partnerships and follow-on funding. We help portfolio companies expand internationally and reach commercial milestones.

  • Strategic partnerships with hospitals and pharma companies
  • Follow-on funding support from VCs and strategic investors
  • International expansion across our hub network
  • Clinical validation and pilot programme facilitation

For: portfolio companies with product-market fit, ventures seeking scale and international expansion.
