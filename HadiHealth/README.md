# Hadi Health

Business plan and brand of Hadi Health, the bioERGOtech initiative that sells and runs
Frankura and VIS Pharma Compass in Italian hospitals and regions. Work in progress.

```
plan/build.py      builds the plan from plan.html, theme.css and model.py (--pdf for the PDFs)
plan/assets/       fonts (Mulish, OFL), map of Italy, bioERGOtech logo
brand/A, brand/B   the two logo proposals
sources/           source documents used for the figures
notes/             facts and sources
```
